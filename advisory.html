TECHNICAL BLUEPRINT

Mercury Verado V12 (600 HP) Propulsion Architecture

Historically, luxury vessels over 50 feet relied exclusively on heavy inboard diesel engines (MTU, MAN, Caterpillar), sacrificing substantial interior cabin and tender garage space. The introduction of the naturally aspirated 7.6L V12 Mercury Verado outboard changed superyacht design forever.

Steerable Gearcase: The engine head remains static while only the lower unit turns, enabling ultra-tight multi-engine transom spacing (27 inches center-to-center) and 45-degree steering articulation.
Two-Speed Transmission: First gear delivers explosive hole-shot torque to plane heavy center consoles and tenders effortlessly; second gear shifts seamlessly for high-speed luxury cruising up to 60+ knots.
Space & OpEx Recovery: Eliminating inboard engine rooms frees up to 250 square feet of aft volume for beach clubs, water toy garages, or additional VIP staterooms.
